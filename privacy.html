Signing in to the family area

The private family area at family.nikhilharsh.com uses Google sign-in through Cloudflare Access. When you sign in, we receive your name and email address from Google. We use them only to check that you are on the family's list of people allowed in. We do not see your Google password, and we do not read your email, contacts, files or anything else in your Google account.

Paws & Keys

The game at play.nikhilharsh.com saves each player's progress (name, coins, lessons, pets and similar game data) so it works on any computer. Online Battle shows a player's name and fighter to other players who are online at the same time. There is no chat.

Visitor statistics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It does not use cookies or track you across other websites.

Sharing
